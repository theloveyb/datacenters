Refinancing Due Diligence — Associate Checklist

Reminder: Fiera Real Estate is the borrower in this refinancing. The items below are oriented toward acting for the borrower, not the lender.

Entity & Authorization

  • Confirm exact borrowing entity (SPE, LP, or Fiera RE Investments Ltd. directly)
  • Obtain corporate profile report (ON/QC registry) for borrower entity
  • Review constating documents (articles / LP agreement) for borrowing powers
  • Obtain board / GP resolution authorizing the refinancing and signing officers
  • Confirm no change-of-control or assignment-of-interest restrictions in existing mortgage
  • Check if parent guarantee (Fiera Capital Corp.) is required by lender

Loan & Security

  • Review existing mortgage / charge — confirm outstanding balance and maturity
  • Obtain discharge statement from existing lender (if full refinance)
  • Review fund-level LTV / borrowing covenants (CORE Fund: low-leverage policy)
  • Confirm property title — subsearch / title insurance requirements
  • Review survey, environmental (Phase I/II), and zoning compliance
  • Check for existing subordinate financing (mezz, PACE, etc.)
  • Review any intercreditor or co-ownership agreements
  • Confirm PPSA registrations and any existing security interests

Property & Tenant

  • Obtain rent roll and estoppel certificates from major tenants
  • Review material leases — assignment / subordination provisions
  • Confirm insurance coverage meets lender requirements
  • Confirm property tax status (current / arrears)
  • Review property management agreement (any lender-consent triggers)
  • Check municipal compliance — building permits, occupancy certificates

Closing & Post-Closing

  • Prepare borrower’s certificate (reps & warranties, no default)
  • Review lender’s commitment letter / term sheet for all conditions precedent
  • Coordinate discharge of existing mortgage (timing with new advance)
  • Prepare opinion letter (capacity, authorization, enforceability, real property)
  • Register new charge / mortgage on title (Teraview in Ontario)
  • File PPSA financing change statements as needed
  • Confirm compliance with ESG / sustainability covenants if applicable
